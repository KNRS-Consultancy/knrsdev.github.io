What we do

Six services, one point of contact

Each one stands on its own. Most clients start with a single need and hand us the next piece once they see how the first one goes.

ST

Permanent, contract and temporary hires — sourced, screened and presented with a shortlist you can actually act on. We handle job descriptions, offer paperwork and the first weeks of onboarding.

  • Permanent & contract roles
  • Shortlisting and interview panels
  • Offers, joining and onboarding
MP

Manpower supply

Skilled, semi-skilled and general workforce in the numbers you need, when the site or shift needs them. Deployment is tracked, documented and compliant with local labour rules.

RR

Recruitment referrals

A vetted referral network for roles outside our desk or outside your region. You get a warm introduction to a recruiter who already works that market — not a cold list.

CF

Opening a new company

From name reservation to the day you can legally invoice. We prepare the filings, walk the paperwork through, and tell you plainly what each step costs and how long it takes.

  • Bank account and compliance setup

Introductions to buyers, vendors and partners inside our network. We only make a referral when both sides genuinely fit — a bad introduction costs us more than it earns.

Website assistance

A fast, clean website you own outright, plus the boring parts kept alive: domain, hosting, business email, backups and updates. Built to load quickly on a phone.

How we work

Four steps, in this order

The sequence matters. We do not quote before we understand the requirement, and we do not disappear after delivery.

  1. 01

    You tell us the requirement

    A call or a message. Roles, headcount, timelines, budget, or just the problem you are trying to solve. No forms to fill before a human reads it.

  2. 02

    We come back with a plan and a price

    Scope, timeline and cost in writing, including what is not included. If a piece is better handled by someone in our referral network, we say so.

  3. 03

    We deliver, and you can see it happening

    Regular updates while work is in progress — candidates shortlisted, filings submitted, pages published. One person stays accountable throughout.

  4. 04

    We stay reachable afterwards

    Replacement support on placements, renewal reminders on registrations, and edits on websites. Most of our work now comes from clients we started with years ago.

About KNRS

Built for owners who are doing five jobs at once

KNRS Consultancy started with a simple observation: a growing business needs a recruiter, a manpower supplier, a company-registration agent and a web developer — and ends up managing four relationships, four invoices and four sets of excuses.

We put those services under one roof. The same team that hires your staff can register your entity and keep your website online. That means fewer handovers, one number to call, and someone who already knows your business when the next requirement comes up.

We work with startups taking on their first employees, contractors staffing sites at short notice, and established firms opening in a new market.
